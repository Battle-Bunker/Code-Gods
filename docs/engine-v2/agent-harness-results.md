What it shows:

- **Solo, the examples climb**, at 3–18 bits per 100 ⚡: Crosswise Threes/fold-line (17.8), Two Pair/primes and

  pairs and rows are far from 9 and from mirrors).
- **Solo, the points-aware starters climb less** (0–8 bits per 100 ⚡), by design: alone, a board scores no points, so
  below their 300 ⚡ reserve they keep their energy (and their skipped turns still pay for reading the board).

  tables; Simple ≈ 2,280 / 430 / 1,360; Example ≈ 1,000 / 530 / 1,700; Bold ≈ 1,480 / 76 / 670; Thorough ≈ 95 / 360 /
  185. The points-aware starters do not yet beat Naive: in these tables every rule pulls the shared boards somewhere
  else, so holding back below the reserve mostly forgoes gains, and Thorough (pair moves only 2 apart, many quotes)
  spends its energy on reads and skips. The points design needs another pass (see agents.md: unify with the line
