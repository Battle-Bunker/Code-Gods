# Extra instructions for this walk (in addition to PERSONA.md)

- Before you start a game: the friend who sent you the link also said "when you make your room, pick the setup called
  «SETUP»" (the exact name is in your brief). Find where a room's setup is chosen on the room page and pick it, as a person
  told this would. If your brief says "no setup instruction", change nothing.
- Play the whole first game to its natural end (about a minute of planning and five minutes of play). Do not end the game
  yourself. After your first agent is running, keep behaving as your persona: look at the boards, try to understand what is
  happening, and try the one small change the screen suggests if your persona would.
- In addition to the five timings, answer these in your report, from what you actually saw and did (quote the screen):
  a. Did anything on the boards look out of date, dimmed, marked old/stale, or otherwise "not live"? What did the screen say,
     at what clock time, and what did you think it meant at that moment (in character)?
  b. Did you understand it correctly later? What on the screen explained it?
  c. Did the screen offer you a way to refresh or look at a board for a price? Did you use it? Why or why not? What did it cost,
     and did you understand where that came from?
  d. Did you at any point act on, or draw a conclusion from, a board that turned out to be out of date? Did it stop you from
     doing something, or make you wait?
  e. Could you tell which boards your own agent had changed, and what they look like now?
  f. One sentence: did looking at the boards feel natural, confusing, or annoying?
- Keep the report under 45 lines.
